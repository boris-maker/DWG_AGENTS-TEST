DeepWebGallery is not a gallery in the traditional sense. No fixed physical space. No stable staff roster. No single institutional voice.

It is a research-first operation — a recurring convergence of individuals (artists, engineers, theorists, curators, writers) who synchronize around explicit programs and projects. The organization is itself as much an output as what it produces.

The operative logic is decentralized collaboration. People work under aliases. There is no single author. Operating on the deep web is not a technical detail — it is a position. An argument made through presence about what online space is, who controls it, and what symbolic power looks like when those systems are made visible or deliberately evaded.
