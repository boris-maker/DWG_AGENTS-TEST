◆ What Counts as a Vulnerability
Symbolic
Register A — Conceptual / Structural
A structural contradiction, power leak, governance exploit, or curatorial blindspot in the institution's logic. Institutional critique as vulnerability disclosure.
  • Corporate board composition + sponsorship conflicts
  • Canon formation: whose work enters the collection?
  • Ticket pricing vs. "public institution" mandate
  • Labor disputes and staff organizing
  • Expansion history and franchise logic
Technical
Register B — White Hat / Digital Infrastructure
A real security or opsec finding in the institution's public digital infrastructure. No exploitation. No harm. Submit through CICFA — we handle responsible disclosure.
  • Public website and API endpoints
  • Ticketing system opsec
  • Digital collection infrastructure
  • Subdomain enumeration / cert exposure
  • Public data leaks or misconfigured assets

Both types are valid. A single submission can combine them.

◆ How to Submit
01
Write the disclosure
Document, visual diagram, or web artifact. 1–3 pages. Must be specific enough to verify — name the vulnerability, explain the mechanism, show the evidence.
02
Submit via the link below
Include your ETH wallet address for payout. Register B findings are held in confidence for 30 days before public archival. The institution is notified by formal disclosure letter (itself a CICFA artifact).
03
Jury reviews — you get paid
The jury votes by wallet signature. Simple majority. On approval, the full ETH pool transfers to your address. Your finding is archived. The archive is the work.
◆ Unlock Conditions
A submission is valid if all of the following are true:
01 It discloses a genuine vulnerability in the target's institutional architecture Symbolic, structural, or technical — both registers qualify
02 It follows the submission format and contains sufficient detail to verify the finding Vague claims do not qualify — the disclosure must be specific and documented
03 It passes review by the CICFA Jury (simple majority) Jury composition published below — no anonymous adjudication
04 [Register B only] Survives 30-day responsible disclosure window without retraction Target institution notified via ransom letter; finding published after 30 days
